Build a complete playable web + mobile game titled IRON TIDE. Ship a working app on first pass, not a wireframe, not a demo, not a slideshow.

LEGAL
- Original game. Title is IRON TIDE only.
- Do not use the name P.T.O., Pacific Theater of Operations, Teitoku no Ketsudan, or Koei Tecmo trademarks.
- Do not copy Koei menus, portraits, music, box art, or ROM code.
- Public WWII facts are allowed: historical ship names, admirals, ports, 1 Nov 1941 and 7 Dec 1941.

GOAL
A full Pacific naval grand-strategy campaign you can play in mobile Safari and on desktop. Same job as a 16-bit Pacific admiral game: two sides, fuel, yards, named fleets, oil ports, landings, 4-hour staff clock.

PLATFORM
- Single-page web app. Touch first. 44px minimum targets. Safe-area insets. Works in iPhone Safari and desktop Chrome.
- Add to Home Screen / standalone display.
- Autosave to localStorage after every order and every watch.
- No account, no backend.

CLOCK
- Default turn is 4 hours (one watch).
- Buttons: End watch (+4h), +1 day, +1 week.
- +1 day / +1 week only skip empty time. Player owns those buttons. Do not replace the 4-hour clock with week-only turns.

CAMPAIGN
- Sides: United States or Japan.
- Scenarios: Storm Warning (1 Nov 1941 08:00, peace until guns or 7 Dec 06:00) and Sunday Morning (7 Dec 1941 06:00, war on, opening strike if US).
- Difficulty: Cadet / Admiral / Iron. Iron starts Japan with less fuel.
- 23 bases at least, including Pearl Harbor, Tokyo, Kure, Truk, Manila, Singapore, Sydney, Midway, Wake, Guam, Saipan, Kwajalein, Palau, Hong Kong, Takao, Rabaul, Port Moresby, Darwin, San Francisco, San Diego, Dutch Harbor, Palembang, Balikpapan.
- Palembang and Balikpapan are OIL ports. Japan must take them or fuel dies.
- Hubs: Pearl, Tokyo, Truk, Manila, Singapore, Sydney.

FLEETS
- 6 US and 6 Japanese task forces. Named historical ships.
- US: Battle Line at Pearl (Arizona, Pennsylvania, California, Tennessee, Maryland, West Virginia, Nevada, Oklahoma), Enterprise TF, Lexington TF, Saratoga Force, Asiatic Fleet (Houston, Boise, Langley), Pacific Subs.
- Japan: Kido Butai (Akagi, Kaga, Soryu, Hiryu, Shokaku, Zuikaku), Battle Force (Nagato, Mutsu, Kongo, Hiei, Kirishima, Haruna), Southern Force with transports, South Seas, Fourth Fleet, Sixth Fleet subs.
- Named admirals: Kimmel, Halsey, Fitch, Fletcher, Hart, Nagumo, Yamamoto, Kondo, Inoue.

SYSTEMS THAT MUST WORK
- Tap fleet, Move, tap destination. Transit time from distance and speed, in watches. Fuel cost.
- Patrol, Strike/bombard, Invade (needs AP transport), Repair in a friendly yard.
- Combat auto-resolves with an after-action report: air then guns. Surprise bonus for the 7 Dec strike on the Pearl battle line. Carriers Enterprise and Lexington are not in Pearl.
- Shipyards: spend steel, wait real weeks (CV ~18 weeks = 756 watches). Show remaining weeks.
- Production: weekly fuel/steel from owned bases, paid prorated per 4-hour watch (1/42 of a week). Do not pay a full week of income every watch.
- Fog: enemy fleets hidden unless nearby or intel spent.
- AI plays the other side: Japan drives south for oil and Manila/Singapore; US holds then island-hops toward Truk/Saipan. AI may not act every single watch — plan on 08:00 / 16:00 / 00:00.
- Win only after war has started AND one of: take enemy capital (Tokyo or Pearl), hold 5 of 6 hubs, or sink every enemy CV and BB.
- CRITICAL: US starts holding Pearl+Manila+Singapore+Sydney. That is 4 hubs. Do NOT use hold 4 hubs as victory or America wins on watch one.

UI
- Title, setup, then map + bottom sheet + dock (Map, Fleets, Yards, Intel, +4h).
- Dark naval look. Brass + navy. Original art only.
- How-to panel on first launch, dismissible.
- War diary on Intel tab.

ACCEPTANCE
- Boot to title in under 2 seconds.
- Start US Storm Warning. End one watch. Game is still running. No victory popup.
- Move Enterprise toward Midway. Transit counts down on later watches.
- Start Sunday Morning as US. Opening strike report appears. Enterprise still exists.
- Start as Japan. Palembang and Balikpapan visible and marked oil.
- Save, reload, campaign continues.
- Usable with one thumb on a 390-wide phone screen.
