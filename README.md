# IRON TIDE — Pacific 1941

Original WWII Pacific naval grand-strategy game. **Not affiliated with Koei, Koei Tecmo, P.T.O., or Teitoku no Ketsudan.**

## Play on iPhone tonight

1. Open this file on your phone: [index.html](https://github.com/JackHemmert3113/iron-tide/blob/main/index.html)
2. Tap the raw file viewer is awkward — better path: enable GitHub Pages on this repo (`Settings → Pages → Deploy from main`) then open:
   - `https://jackhemmert3113.github.io/iron-tide/`
3. In Safari: Share → **Add to Home Screen**. It will launch like an app.

Until Pages is on, open the raw HTML via a gist-style preview or copy `index.html` to Files and open in Safari.

## What this is (legal closeness)

This matches the **genre depth** of 16-bit Pacific naval sims:
- Full campaign from 1 November 1941 (war not yet declared)
- Sunday Morning start (7 December 1941)
- Historical short scenarios (Coral Sea, Midway, Solomons, Marianas, Leyte, Okinawa)
- United States or Japan
- Fuel, materials, shipyards, airfields
- Named historical ships and 24 Pacific bases including Palembang and Balikpapan
- Fleets, search, auto-resolved battles with after-action reports
- Building replacement hulls, fatigue, experience, political capital
- Win by holding 4 of 6 hubs or destroying the enemy CV+BB line

It does **not** copy anyone’s title, box art, menus, portraits, music, or ROM.
